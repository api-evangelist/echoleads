# EchoLeads

> EchoLeads is an autonomous AI voice sales platform that automates outbound calling, inbound lead response, WhatsApp follow-ups, Instagram lead capture, and appointment scheduling — without human intervention. Built for B2B sales teams across real estate, insurance, SaaS, healthcare, financial services, and e-commerce. Based in Hyderabad, India. Backed by Google for Startups and T-Hub (DST, Government of India).

## Platform — core agents

- [AI Calling Agent](https://echoleads.ai/agents/ai-cold-calling): Outbound and inbound AI voice agent for lead qualification, cold calling, and demo booking. Human-like voice, real-time BANT qualification, CRM sync.
- [WhatsApp Agent](https://echoleads.ai/agents/ai-whatsapp): AI-powered WhatsApp sales agent for post-call follow-ups, reminders, and qualification sequences via WhatsApp Business API.
- [Instagram Agent](https://echoleads.ai/agents/instagram): Captures and qualifies leads from Instagram DMs in real time, routes to voice pipeline.
- [Customer Care Agent](https://echoleads.ai/agents/ai-customer-care): AI agent for inbound support calls, query resolution, and callback management.

## Platform — features

- [Lead Scoring](https://echoleads.ai/features/ai-smart-lead-scoring): Real-time intent scoring during calls. Routes high-intent leads to human reps automatically.
- [Analytics & Reporting](https://echoleads.ai/features/ai-analytics-reporting): Call performance, conversion rates, lead quality trends.
- [Enterprise Security](https://echoleads.ai/features/ai-enterprise-security): Encrypted call infrastructure, data ownership, role-based access.
- [API Documentation](https://echoleads.ai/resources/api-documentation): REST API for custom integrations.
